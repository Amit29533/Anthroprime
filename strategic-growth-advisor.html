Why this exists

Why every founder needs an external perspective.

No matter how capable we are, we cannot objectively see the system we are inside. As a founder grows the business, they become immersed in customers, employees, delivery, hiring, cash flow, firefighting, investor expectations, and daily decisions.

Over time, the business begins to consume the founder's attention. The result isn't a lack of intelligence — it's a lack of perspective.

The role of a Strategic Growth Advisor is not to work harder than the founder. It is to help the founder see what they cannot see from within the business.
